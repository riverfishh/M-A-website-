Run like a deal team

The Nova M&A Club was founded at Nova SBE in 2023 to give students hands-on exposure to mergers and acquisitions.

Members join as analysts and associates in one of five departments, work in small teams under team leaders and department heads, and learn by analysing real transactions.

Our history

  1. 2023The M&A Club is founded at Nova SBE.
  2. 2024The Nova M&A Club brand is consolidated.
  3. 2025The club is rebranded.
  4. 2026Five departments, 37 members and more than 130 applications in the fall intake.

Our values

Integrity, analytical rigour and a shared ambition to grow as leaders in finance, driven by collaboration, curiosity and professionalism.

Five departments

Research, Relations, Operations, Marketing and Legal, each led by its own head.

Our mission

To build real deal-making skills through practical analysis, shared knowledge and professional networking.

What the club does

Research on live deals, sessions with practitioners, and the legal side of transactions.

Deal research

Our Research department works in four teams and turns the week's M&A activity into briefs, posts and papers.

  • Sector teams

    Three teams cover their own sectors in depth.

  • Weekly deal coverage

    One team tracks the week's deals, published as a breakdown on Instagram.

  • Research papers

    Three papers each semester.

  • Deal briefs

    Each brief profiles both companies and tests whether the deal would be accretive to the buyer's earnings.

Sessions with practitioners

Workshops, case studies and speaker events with banks, funds and advisors. Before each session our researchers prepare a pre-read; afterwards we write up what we learned.

  • Workshops

    Hands-on sessions with banks, such as our BNP Paribas workshop.

  • Case studies

    Working through a real case with practitioners, like our session with Carlyle.

  • Speaker events

    Talks with live Q&A, like our online event with IMAP.

The legal side of deals

  • Learning sessions

  • Law-firm contacts

Run the first test from our briefs

In an all-stock deal, the buyer's earnings per share rise when the buyer trades at a higher P/E than the target.

Change the two numbers. They start with Pfizer and Exelixis from our Spring 2026 brief.

The test assumes the deal is paid for entirely in stock, with no acquisition premium, no synergies and no transaction costs.

Accretive

For every $1 of its own earnings, the buyer issues shares worth $20.54. It acquires the target's earnings at only $15.82 per $1.

Target earnings yield6.32%

Partner with the club

If your firm wants to meet Nova SBE students who are serious about M&A, we would like to hear from you. We work with banks, funds and advisors on sessions built around your team's expertise.

Propose a session

  • Visibility and reach

    Engage directly with Nova SBE students, share your insights with an active student audience and take part in high-visibility events.

  • Sessions built together

    We co-create each session with you, so students learn from real-world insight and your team gets a direct exchange with motivated students.

  • An early look at talent

    Meet motivated, M&A-focused students early, spot promising candidates and build connections that last.

Formats we can run with you

  • Speaker events and panels
  • Technical workshops
  • Case studies and case competitions
  • Firm and team presentations
  • Online sessions with live Q&A
  • Legal learning sessions on SPAs, due diligence and closing

How it works

  1. Tell us what you have in mind

    Send a short note with the topic, format and rough timing. Our Relations team picks it up and stays your point of contact.

  2. Shape the session together

    We agree on the format, date and audience, and our researchers prepare a pre-read where it helps.

  3. We run the logistics

    Scheduling, promotion, registrations and the day itself are handled by our Relations, Marketing and Operations teams.

  4. Follow-up

    Afterwards we write up the key insights, collect feedback and follow up with you.
