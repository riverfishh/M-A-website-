Run like a deal team

The Nova M&A Club was founded at Nova SBE in 2023 to give students hands-on exposure to mergers and acquisitions.

Members join as analysts and associates in one of five departments, work in small teams under team leaders and department heads, and learn by analysing real transactions.

Our history

  1. 2023The M&A Club is founded at Nova SBE.
  2. 2024The Nova M&A Club brand is consolidated.
  3. 2025The club is rebranded.
  4. 2026Five departments, 37 members and more than 130 applications in the fall intake.

Our values

Integrity, analytical rigour and a shared ambition to grow as leaders in finance, driven by collaboration, curiosity and professionalism.

Five departments

Research, Relations, Operations, Marketing and Legal, each led by its own head.

Our mission

To build real deal-making skills through practical analysis, shared knowledge and professional networking.

What the club does

Research on live deals, sessions with practitioners, and the legal side of transactions.

Deal research

Our Research department works in four teams and turns the week's M&A activity into briefs, posts and papers.

  • Sector teams

    Three teams cover their own sectors in depth.

  • Weekly deal coverage

    One team tracks the week's deals, published as a breakdown on Instagram.

  • Research papers

    Three papers each semester.

  • Deal briefs

    Each brief profiles both companies and tests whether the deal would be accretive to the buyer's earnings.

Follow our weekly deal coverage on Instagram (opens in a new tab)

Sessions with practitioners

Workshops, case studies and speaker events with banks, funds and advisors. Before each session our researchers prepare a pre-read; afterwards we write up what we learned.

  • Workshops

    Hands-on sessions with banks, such as our BNP Paribas workshop.

  • Case studies

    Working through a real case with practitioners, like our session with Carlyle.

  • Speaker events

    Talks with live Q&A, like our online event with IMAP.

The legal side of deals

Our Legal department, made up of law and management Master's students, adds the legal angle to the deals we cover.

  • Legal angle

    A legal read of the deals in our coverage.

  • Learning sessions

    Share purchase agreements, due diligence and closing.

  • Law-firm contacts

    Building contacts with law firms for speakers and cases.

Life in the club

Beyond the work, there is a club: dinners, a run club, padel, beach days and get-togethers after meetings.

  • Onboarding

    New members start with an onboarding session.

  • Alumni roundtables

    M&A roundtables with club alumni.

  • Workshops with former presidents

    Practical guidance from the people who led the club before.
